Why participate

Low-cost land is not the same as a low-cost project. The Academy helps nonprofit leaders decide whether, when and how real estate serves their mission, and how to carry a project from idea to long-term stewardship responsibly.

Nonprofit infrastructure

The governance, policies and financial footing real estate activity demands.

Mission alignment

Clarity on whether owning, developing or partnering truly advances your community's needs.

Responsible acquisition

Know what you are taking on before you request a property.

Sustainable delivery

Projects that hold up long after the deed is recorded.

What you'll learn

Eight integrated modules over two live half days. Applied and hands-on, built around a Shelby County Land Bank case study, with skills that transfer to any nonprofit real estate or community development project.

Featured topic

Know Before You Convey

Land Bank properties are a powerful tool for nonprofits, and they come as-is. Learn what to look for before you request one, while you can still walk away.

Know the land. Understand the liability. Build the team. Test the feasibility. Then pursue the conveyance.

  • What a Land Bank deed does, and does not, give you
  • Taxes, title and ownership questions to answer early
  • Testing a property before you commit to it
  • The partners to bring in, and when

Educational content only, not legal, tax or title advice.
