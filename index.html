10-minute team pitch · every member speaks

Pitch deck dueTue, Dec 112:00 noon Central. No exceptions.
ShowcaseTue, Dec 8Crosstown Theatre. Present to the judges.
Bring with you5 printed copiesof your full development package
Read the team instructionsDownload pitch template (.pptx)
Deck vs. package. Your slides tell the story and show the key numbers in 10 minutes. Every supporting detail, from full budgets to comps, bids and letters, goes in the printed development package.

Template rules every team follows

  1. Use the cover, the 12 content slides and the closing slide, in the same order with the same titles.
  2. Replace every gray bracketed placeholder with your own content. Delete any placeholder you do not use.
  3. One slide per section. Put extra charts or detail in an appendix after the closing slide; it will not be presented.
  4. Brand it your way with your own colors, fonts and logo. Keep the layout, slide order, titles and content blocks. No text under 24 point.
  5. Every number on a slide must match your development package exactly. Date and cite every market figure.
  6. Label status honestly: Secured, In progress or Needed. Never present a commitment you do not have.

Slide by slide: your 10-minute run of show

Rehearse to 9:30 to leave a cushion. Assign a speaker to each slide and practice the handoffs.

  • Cover0:15

    Team name, project name, address, what you will build and for whom, team members

  • 1. Project at a glance0:45

    Address, what you will build, who it serves, total cost, your ask

    Use the Lab: Project Summary →

  • 4. Zoning, permits and approvals0:30

  • Closing slide

What the judges look for

Strengths

  • A real site and a clear product
  • A buyer who can afford the home
  • Numbers that tie out across deck and package
  • A confident, coordinated team

Challenges

  • Gaps in cost, approvals or market evidence
  • Unrealistic timelines or prices
  • Funding that is assumed, not sourced

Recommendations

  • Judges will suggest next steps
  • Show what technical help you need
  • Ask them questions on slide 12

Each judge completes a feedback form on your project’s strengths, challenges and recommendations. Build your pitch so those answers are easy to write.

How to register your parking

Option 1

Premium Parking App
  1. Open the Premium Parking App.
  2. Search for location P2725.
  3. Select Regular Space and the 8-Hour Rate.
  4. Enter your vehicle’s license plate number.
  5. At checkout, select Apply Promo Code.
  6. Enter EDA2026 to reduce the rate to $10.
  7. Complete your parking registration and save a screenshot of your confirmation.

Option 2

Scan the garage QR code
  1. Scan the QR code displayed on garage signage.
  2. Open the link and enter your phone number.
  3. Enter your vehicle’s license plate number.
  4. Select Regular Space and the 8-Hour Rate.
  5. Apply promo code EDA2026.
  6. Complete registration and save your confirmation.

Option 3

Text-to-Pay
  1. Text P2725 to 504504.
  2. Open the link received by text.
  3. Enter your phone number and license plate number.
  4. Select Regular Space and the 8-Hour Rate.
  5. Apply promo code EDA2026.
  6. Complete registration and save your confirmation.

Important parking reminders

  • DO NOT park in STAR SPACES. These are marked with stars painted on the pavement and signage on the garage walls.
  • The promo code will NOT work at the physical payment machines. Use one of the three mobile payment options above.
  • Double-check your license plate number before completing registration.
  • Save a screenshot of your parking confirmation.
  • Follow the Academy team’s instructions regarding payment of the discounted parking charge.

City of Memphis Down Payment Assistance: what developers should know

From the City’s current FY26 Guidelines (effective July 1, 2025). Guidelines · Buyer application package

Pricing your homes

  • Price cap: $300,000 for Citywide DPA, MHA and SHAPE buyers (new or existing homes). $350,000 for the Homebuyer Incentive Programs (HIP).
  • Award: up to 10% of the sales price, capped at $25,000. A $215,000 home qualifies for up to $21,500.
  • Sales price can’t exceed the appraised value. An appraisal gap can sink a DPA deal, so price to your comps.
  • Single-family homes inside Memphis city limits only.

Who your buyers are

  • Citywide DPA: first-time buyers at or below 80% AMI (HUD HOME income limits).
  • MHA and SHAPE: current or recent MHA residents and Housing Choice Voucher holders.
  • HIP Citizens: up to 200% AMI, but only in designated ZIP codes. Ask HCD for the current list.
  • HIP Police, Fire and Memphis/Shelby County Schools: no income limit, anywhere in the city.
  • Buyers put in at least 1% of the price ($100 for MHA and SHAPE), need one month of house payment in the bank, and a 550+ credit score.

Building to pass inspection

  • Citywide, MHA and SHAPE homes must pass a City inspection to UPCS standards (health and safety, major systems, lead-based paint, accessibility) before the file goes to DPA. HIP has no City inspection.
  • Pre-1978 rehabs: lead-based paint rules apply. Use EPA Lead-Safe Certified firms and lead-safe work practices.
  • Flood zone homes require flood insurance, which raises the buyer’s payment.

Timeline and closing

  • The buyer needs 8 hours of in-person counseling from a HUD-approved agency before the lender submits. Online classes don’t count. Get buyers started early.
  • Buyers must use a DPA-approved lender with a fixed-rate, 30-year first mortgage (15 to 30 for HIP). Conventional, FHA, THDA or VA.
  • Lenders submit paper files only, and funds go first come, first served to complete files. Build review time into your closing date.
  • No cash back to the buyer at closing. DPA won’t pay for a rate buydown, but a buydown can count toward the buyer’s 1%.

What the buyer signs

  • The DPA is a deferred loan with no monthly payments, secured by a second lien. It is forgiven after the 5-year affordability period.
  • The buyer must live in the home the whole time. Renting it out is a default, so DPA buyers can’t be investors.
  • If the home sells early, the City recaptures the balance from net proceeds.
  • Layering: if your project also uses City HOME development money, total HOME dollars in the unit can’t exceed HUD’s per-unit subsidy limit.
